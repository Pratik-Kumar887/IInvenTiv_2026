          - ## About the Event
         
          - **IInvenTiv 2026 – Global Innovation Showcase** was hosted at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad on **6th–7th April 2026**. IInvenTiv is a flagship annual R&D fair organized by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, that brings together IITs, NITs, IISc, IIMs, IISERs, and top NIRF-ranked institutes to showcase indigenous innovations and strengthen India's research-to-technology ecosystem in line with **Make in India** and **Atmanirbhar Bharat**.
         
          - The 2026 edition was jointly organized by IIT Kharagpur, IIT (BHU) Varanasi, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, IIT Patna, and IIT Bhubaneswar.
