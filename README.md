# IInvenTiv 2026 – Visit Report & Innovation Summary

This repository contains a visit report prepared for the **IInvenTiv 2026 – Global Innovation Showcase**, hosted at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad on 6th–7th April 2026.

IInvenTiv is the Ministry of Education's flagship annual R&D fair that brings together IITs, NITs, IISc, IIMs, IISERs, and other leading institutes to showcase indigenous innovations and strengthen India's research-to-technology ecosystem under the **Make in India** and **Atmanirbhar Bharat** initiatives.

## About the Report

The report documents **13 distinct innovations** observed during the visit, with a focus on their relevance to mining operations and indigenous technological advancement. It covers innovations across five thematic areas:

- Earth Science
- Health Care
- Materials Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Semiconductor Technology & Chip Design

Featured innovations include distributed fiber optic sensing systems, enhanced gravity mineral concentrators, conformal MIMO antenna systems for open-cast mining UAVs, molecular integration adhesive technologies, and other advancements in mineral processing, monitoring, UAV communications, and digital mining.
